Project brief

Household battery buffering

The upstream edition included a Pennsylvania permitting and interconnection guide. Those rules do not apply in North Carolina and were not copied. This page lists official North Carolina starting points only.

Official places to begin

  • N.C. Utilities Commission — state utility regulation, including interconnection dockets and orders
  • Your retail electric utility’s published interconnection or net-metering procedures (the utility named on the electric bill). Do not assume one utility’s process applies statewide.
  • The North Carolina State Building Code, administered through the Office of State Fire Marshal / Office of State Fire Marshal building code staff and the local inspections department
  • N.C. Department of Environmental Quality for environmental permitting questions that may apply to a specific site
