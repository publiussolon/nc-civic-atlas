    Sources and map coverage

    The map covers 2,655 precinct polygons across all 100 counties for the November 5, 2024 presidential election. The official NCSBE results file lists 2,658 real precincts plus administrative reporting units for early, absentee, and provisional ballots. The two sets do not correspond one for one. Mapped vote totals come from the New York Times precinct dataset, which nearly matches the official statewide US President total. Official Real Precinct = Y rows generally omit those administrative ballots, so they are not painted alone onto Election Day precinct shapes.
