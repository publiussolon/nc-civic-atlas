# North Carolina district officeholders

Checked 2026-11-24. The map lists the officeholders in the published 2025–2026 North Carolina General Assembly and the 119th U.S. House directories. Each entry links to the official member profile or office website. Party is the affiliation listed by that chamber.

| Chamber | Districts listed | Directory |

| North Carolina House | 120 | [House Members](https://www.ncleg.gov/Members/MemberList/H) |
| North Carolina Senate | 50 | [Senate Members](https://www.ncleg.gov/Members/MemberList/S) |
| U.S. House, North Carolina delegation | 14 | [U.S. House Directory of Representatives](https://www.house.gov/representatives) |

The district lists use the atlas’s [2026-11-24 state roster](../../nc-legislators.json). Members serving more than one county are counted once by district. The federal list uses the North Carolina table in the U.S. House directory. Each directory supplied an officeholder for every numbered district at the time checked.

Seated 2025-2026 North Carolina General Assembly roster as shown on the official NCGA member tables, plus sitting U.S. House members of the 119th Congress as listed on house.gov. November 2026 election winners are not assumed seated before the next term in January 2027.

Sitting officeholders can differ from November 2026 election winners. The map uses this dated roster alongside the November 5, 2024 presidential election.

The official House member table lists party as “U” for Districts 99 (Nasif Majeed) and 106 (Carla D. Cunningham). That code is stored as published; it is not expanded here.
