State finance

State tax collections, and who was governor

Total state tax collections are from the U.S. Census Bureau State Tax Collections historical database, field C105, in thousands of nominal dollars. Governor years are the calendar-year ranges published by NCpedia (State Library of North Carolina / Office of Archives and History, revised September 2025). Mid-year inaugurations are not dated in that list, so a fiscal year may overlap more than one governor. These figures are not inflation-adjusted and are not a complete picture of state spending.
