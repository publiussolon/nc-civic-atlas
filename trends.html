Resident population

The Census publication Population of States and Counties of the United States: 1790 to 1990 lists 393,751 North Carolina residents in 1790 and 6,628,637 in 1990. The April 1, 2020 estimates base is 10,441,392. The July 1, 2025 county-based state estimate is 11,197,968.

Foreign-born residents (ACS benchmark)

The 2024 ACS five-year estimates (2019–2024) count 973,325 foreign-born residents in North Carolina, 9.1% of the 10,730,404 people in that survey estimate (margin of error ±9,858 on the foreign-born count, 90% confidence). This is a survey estimate, not a 2024 headcount, and should not be plotted as a continuous series with earlier decennial nativity counts.

Language other than English at home (ACS benchmark)

Among 10,127,421 North Carolina residents age 5 and over in the same ACS 5-year release, 8,764,888 speak only English at home. The remainder, 1,362,533 people (13.5%), speak a language other than English at home.

ACS 2024 5-year table B16001

Fertility and resident abortion rates

The State Center for Health Statistics, in its 2023 Reported Pregnancies tables, publishes resident fertility and abortion rates per 1,000 females age 15–44. The total fertility rate was 58.4 in 2018 and 56.0 in 2023. The resident abortion rate was 11.3 in 2018, peaked at 13.7 in 2022 in this window, and was 12.7 in 2023. Resident abortion counts and a separately defined 2024 statutory report are on the public-health page.
