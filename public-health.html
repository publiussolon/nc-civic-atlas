2024 statutory report (different series)

The NCDHHS report required by G.S. 90-21.93, submitted February 26, 2026, states that 39,315 intentional terminations of pregnancy were reported from January 1 through December 31, 2024. That is a count of reported procedures, not a 2024 point on the resident SCHS series above, and should not be plotted as one.
