Documentary records

American history through North Carolina records

These entries are pointers to official or documentary editions. They are not a narrative history of the state, and they do not settle disputed legends.

Who could vote?

Read qualifications in the current constitution and in official compilations of earlier constitutions and amendments.

First state constitution and later revisions

The N.C. General Assembly Library compiles the 1776 constitution’s adoption, later conventions, and the 1868 text. A documentary edition of the 1776 constitution is also published by the Yale Avalon Project.
