Program directory

Follow public funding to a local program

These are official program and office locators. They are not a county spending history and not a determination of anyone’s eligibility.

  • Medicaid and health coverage. N.C. Medicaid
  • Food and Nutrition Services (SNAP). NCDHHS Food and Nutrition Services
  • Work First cash assistance. Work First
  • Energy assistance (including LIHEAP). NCDHHS Energy Assistance
  • Child care subsidy and related early childhood programs. Division of Child Development and Early Education
  • Aging and adult services. Division of Aging and Adult Services
  • Local social services offices. Programs above are usually administered through a county department of social services. Use the official NCDHHS local DSS directory when that page lists a county office, and confirm hours with the county.
  • School nutrition. Federal program tables are published by USDA FNS. Local participation and budgets belong to the school district and the N.C. Department of Public Instruction.

Sources checked as official agency pages on November 24, 2026. Program rules change; read the linked agency page for current requirements.
